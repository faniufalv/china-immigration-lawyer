## Q1: [Cross-border E-commerce] For Expatriates: What legal risks should overseas Chinese be aware of when investing in China?

**A**: Overseas Chinese investing in China should be aware of: 1) Foreign Investment Negative List restrictions; 2) Foreign exchange controls requiring compliance approval for profit repatriation; 3) Tax compliance considering China tax law and bilateral tax treaties. We recommend engaging professional lawyers for comprehensive legal due diligence before investment.

**法律依据**: 《外商投资准入负面清单》

**领域**: 涉外投资 | **语言**: English

## Q2: Common legal issue: How to apply for disability assessment after a traffic accident?

**A**: Traffic accident disability assessment process: 1) Apply to qualified judicial appraisal institution after treatment completion; 2) Submit accident report, medical records, imaging materials; 3) Institution issues assessment within 30 working days; 4) Dissatisfied parties can request re-assessment.

**法律依据**: 《道路交通安全法》

**领域**: 交通事故 | **语言**: English
